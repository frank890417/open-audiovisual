Tile dot跨螢幕圓點

Several screens lying on a table become one canvas: one photo places them, one clock and one seed keep them together.幾台平放在桌上的螢幕變成一張畫布：拍一張照片定位，同一個時鐘、同一個種子讓它們一起演。

Try these three things試試這三件事

  1. Run node serve.js, open this page with ?room=desk on three screens, tap Join on each and lay them flat on the table.
  2. On a phone open packages/tile/?room=desk, press Markers, then take one photo from above with every screen in it.
  3. Watch the dot leave one screen and come out of the next one where it really is; move a screen and take the photo again.
  1. 跑 node serve.js，在三台螢幕上打開這一頁並加上 ?room=desk，各點一下 Tap to join，平放在桌上。
  2. 用一支手機打開 packages/tile/?room=desk，按「標記」，從上方拍一張把每台螢幕都拍進去的照片。
  3. 看圓點離開一台螢幕，從下一台它真正所在的位置出來；移動一台螢幕，再拍一次。

Needs: Phone or iPad.需要：手機或 iPad。 No hardware? Use Plays itself.沒有硬體？用它自己會播。

Read these lines讀這幾行

  • main.js:28–35 Every position is a function of the shared time and seed, never a sum of this screen's frames.每個位置都是共同時間與種子的函式，從不累加這台螢幕自己的影格。
  • main.js:65–70 The view says which piece of the shared canvas this screen draws: one setTransform.view 說這台螢幕畫共同畫布的哪一塊：一個 setTransform。
  • main.js:121–141 Join the room, wrap the world, route the camera page's Markers to a param, keep the timeline on the shared clock.加入房間、包住世界、把拍照頁的「標記」接到參數、讓時間軸跟著共同時鐘。
