Chord garden和弦花園

Triads bloom, clusters decay. The world listens to chords, never to keys.和弦語意：三和弦開花，音堆凋零。世界聽的是和弦訊號，從來不看琴鍵。

Try these three things試試這三件事

  1. Play a triad: flowers open along the branches.
  2. Play a cluster of adjacent keys: branches droop and the petals fall.
  3. Do nothing: the gardener plays itself until you touch a key.
  1. 彈一個三和弦：枝條上開出花。
  2. 彈幾個相鄰的琴鍵（音堆）：枝條垂下，花瓣落盡。
  3. 什麼都不做：園丁會自己彈，直到你碰琴鍵。

No hardware needed. On-screen piano · QWERTY keys · Simulated performer.不需要硬體。螢幕鋼琴、電腦鍵盤、模擬演奏者。
