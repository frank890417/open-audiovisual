Projector output投影輸出

An output window for a projector or a second screen, its four corners pinned to the wall, and a clean address for OBS and TouchDesigner.給投影機或第二個螢幕的輸出視窗，四個角釘到牆上的位置，還有給 OBS 和 TouchDesigner 用的乾淨網址。

Try these three things試試這三件事

  1. Press “Open output window”, drag the window onto the projector or a second screen, and press F in it (on one screen it stays a window).
  2. Press E in the output window and drag the four numbered corners: the picture bends to fit; G shows the test pattern bent the same way, and a reload keeps it.
  3. Press Space: the timeline fades the projector out over the last 8 seconds through output/main/brightness, the same param the console's slider moves.
  1. 按「Open output window」，把視窗拖到投影機或第二個螢幕上，在視窗裡按 F（只有一個螢幕時它就是一個視窗）。
  2. 在輸出視窗按 E，拖四個有編號的角：畫面會跟著彎過去。按 G 顯示一樣彎過去的測試圖，重新整理後還在。
  3. 按空白鍵：時間軸在最後 8 秒經過 output/main/brightness 把投影淡出，控台上的滑桿推的也是同一個參數。

No hardware needed. On-screen piano · QWERTY keys.不需要硬體。螢幕鋼琴、電腦鍵盤。
