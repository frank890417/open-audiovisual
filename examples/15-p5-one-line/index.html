OP Configurator (OPC)

On OpenProcessing, if you already name your variables with OPC, the controller is wired to them in order: knobs drive the sliders, pads flip toggles and press buttons. OPC exists only on OpenProcessing; on other pages the lines below have nothing to run on.

在 OpenProcessing 上，如果你本來就用 OPC 宣告變數，控制器會依序接上去：旋鈕推 slider，打擊墊切換 toggle、按 button。OPC 只有 OpenProcessing 有，在其他頁面這幾行沒有東西可接。

OPC.slider('radius', 60, 10, 300);    // knob 1
OPC.slider('speed', 1, 0, 5, 0.1);    // knob 2
OPC.toggle('trails', true);           // pad 1 (flips on each hit)

Don't name an OPC variable after a p5 function (hue, fill, …).

OPC 的變數別取 p5 已有的函式名（hue、fill……）。

No hardware? Three ways in沒有硬體也能玩

🎹 opens a controller on screen (it follows the one you plug in). 📱 Phone inside that panel shows a QR code: scan it and your phone becomes the controller, over the public relay. A real MIDI controller works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox; the browser asks once. Safari and iPad have no Web MIDI, but the screen and the phone still work.

🎹 打開螢幕上的控制器（你接上實體控制器時它會跟著變）。面板裡的 📱 手機 會出現 QR code，掃了手機就是控制器（走公開 relay）。實體 MIDI 控制器在 Chrome、Edge、Firefox 可用，瀏覽器只問一次；Safari 與 iPad 沒有 Web MIDI，螢幕和手機照樣能彈。

The sketch can't tell the three apart. That is the point: write it once.
草圖分不出這三種輸入，這就是重點：寫一次，三種都能彈。
