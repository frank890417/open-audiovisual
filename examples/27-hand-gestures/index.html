Example 27 · Anything can be an instrument範例 27 · 什麼都能當樂器

Hand gestures手勢

What a camera can tell about two hands: gestures, a pinch as an event, how open a hand is, its angle, the distance between the hands, drawn on the hands.鏡頭能從兩隻手讀出什麼：手勢、捏一下、手張開多少、手的角度、兩手的距離，數字直接畫在手上。

Try these three things試試這三件事

  1. Before the camera: two simulated hands open, pinch, make a fist, a victory sign and a thumb up. Watch the numbers on each hand and the signals in the console.
  2. Turn on 🖐 hands and allow the camera. Open and close your right hand: the flower at your fingertip follows. Pinch thumb and index with the other fingers out: sparks.
  3. Make a victory sign with the right hand to turn the colours, a thumb up for confetti, a left fist to let the sparks fall. Move both hands apart and together: the ring.
  1. 鏡頭打開之前：兩隻模擬的手會張開、捏、握拳、比 YA、比讚。看每隻手上的數字，和控台裡的訊號。
  2. 打開 🖐 hands 並允許鏡頭。右手張開、握起：指尖的花跟著開合。拇指捏食指、其他三指張開：噴出火花。
  3. 右手比 YA 換顏色，比讚撒彩紙，左手握拳讓火花落下。兩手拉開、靠近：中間的圓圈跟著變大變小。

Needs: Camera (hands).需要：鏡頭（手）。 No hardware? Use Simulated performer · On-screen piano.沒有硬體？用模擬演奏者、螢幕鋼琴。
