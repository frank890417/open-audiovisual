Example 26 · Anything can be an instrument範例 26 · 什麼都能當樂器

p5 rhythm labp5 節奏實驗台

OAVp5.drums.edit('bd(3,8), …') swaps the rhythm like typing it, OAVp5.drums.undo() goes back a line, OAVp5.drums.strudelUrl opens the line in Strudel; rhythmChanged() says what the line is now.OAVp5.drums.edit('bd(3,8), …') 像打字一樣換節奏，OAVp5.drums.undo() 回上一行，OAVp5.drums.strudelUrl 把這一行帶去 Strudel；rhythmChanged() 告訴你現在是哪一行。

Try these three things試試這三件事

  1. Press 1, 2, 3 or 4: edit() swaps the whole line, the circle and the drum card change at once, and the line is listed under it.
  2. Press Z: undo() goes back one line, as the ↶ on the drum card does. Click a cell or type in the card's line, and rhythmChanged() still tells this page.
  3. Press S: strudelUrl opens the same rhythm at strudel.cc in a new tab.
  1. 按 1、2、3 或 4：edit() 換掉整行，圓圈和鼓機卡馬上跟著變，那一行列在旁邊。
  2. 按 Z：undo() 回到上一行，跟鼓機卡的 ↶ 一樣。點格子或在卡上的那一行打字，rhythmChanged() 一樣會告訴這個頁面。
  3. 按 S：strudelUrl 用新分頁在 strudel.cc 打開同一段節奏。

Needs: QWERTY keys.需要：電腦鍵盤。 No hardware? Use Drum machine.沒有硬體？用鼓機。
