Align投影對位

A wall of several outputs aligned by hand from one layout file: the patterns, the vendor chart and the work take the same path, through one calibration.由好幾路輸出組成的牆，從一份佈局檔手動對位：圖樣、廠商對位圖和作品走同一條路，透過同一份校正檔。

Try these three things試試這三件事

  1. Press Align and drag a yellow corner of the right projector: the picture bends to follow, and stays bent after a reload.
  2. Pick a picture in the list (cells, grid, ident, bars): each one goes out through the same warp as the work.
  3. Press Gray code and step with ◀ ▶: each projector draws its stripes in its own colour channel, never warped.
  1. 按 Align，拖右邊投影機的一個黃色角：畫面跟著彎過去，重新整理之後還是彎的。
  2. 在清單裡選一個畫面（逐格、格線、認機、色條）：每一個都跟作品走同一條 warp 送出去。
  3. 按 Gray code，用 ◀ ▶ 一張一張看：每台投影機在自己的色通道畫自己的條紋，從不經過 warp。

No hardware needed. Plays itself.不需要硬體。它自己會播。

Read these lines讀這幾行

  • main.js:125–134 The work or a pattern, the same warp: a pattern right on the wall means the work is right.作品或圖樣，同一條 warp：圖樣在牆上對了，作品就對。

  • main.js:201–204 The align mode edits the calibration; the page rebuilds the warp when it changes.對位模式改的是校正檔，校正一變，頁面就重建 warp。
