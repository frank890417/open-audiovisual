WebToe stageWebToe 舞台

A WebToe node patch performed as a World through ext() bindings.這個世界是一張 WebToe 節點網路，操作方式接近 TouchDesigner，透過 ext() 綁定來演奏。

Try these three things試試這三件事

  1. Play the piano or tick “simulate performance”: each note pulses the patch.
  2. Press Space to play the timeline: LFO speed and hue follow their lanes through Drift, Swell and Spin.
  3. Drag Zoom in the params panel: the patch follows through ext('zoom').
  1. 彈鋼琴，或勾選 simulate performance：每個音都讓節點網路跳動一下。
  2. 按空白鍵播放時間軸：LFO speed 與 hue 沿著各自的曲線走過 Drift、Swell、Spin。
  3. 在參數面板拖動 Zoom：節點網路透過 ext('zoom') 跟著動。

No hardware needed. On-screen piano · Simulated performer.不需要硬體。螢幕鋼琴、模擬演奏者。
