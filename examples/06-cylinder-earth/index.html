Example 06 · Anything can be an instrument範例 06 · 什麼都能當樂器

Cylinder earthCylinder Earth

A 2020 voice-controlled WebGL sketch; mic bands or the built-in drum machine play it.2020 年用聲音控制的 WebGL 作品。低頻推動柱體，高頻讓旋轉閃爍，大鼓一下就換一組配色。

Try these three things試試這三件事

  1. Turn on 🎤 mic in the L1 panel and clap or sing: lows pump the towers, highs shimmer the spin.
  2. No microphone? The drum machine plays it: a kick turns the palette.
  3. Drag Pump or Density in the params panel to hear and see what each one does.
  1. 在 L1 面板打開 🎤 mic，拍手或唱歌：低頻推動柱體，高頻讓旋轉閃爍。
  2. 沒有麥克風？用鼓機彈它：大鼓一下就換一組配色。
  3. 在參數面板拖動 Pump 或 Density，看每一個參數做什麼。

Needs: Microphone.需要：麥克風。 No hardware? Use Drum machine.沒有硬體？用鼓機。

Read these lines讀這幾行

  • main.js:101–109 Mic bands and drum onsets reach the params, one route each.麥克風的頻段與鼓的起音，各自一條路由接到參數。
  • main.js:111 audio: 'mic' switches the analyzer on; drums: true brings the stand-in.audio: 'mic' 開啟分析器；drums: true 帶來替身。
