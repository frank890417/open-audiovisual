What can this device do?

This page looks at your browser and says what works with open-audiovisual, what needs one more step, and what to do about it. Green works. Yellow works with a step or a limit. Red is blocked.

Nothing runs until you press a button, and nothing leaves your device. MIDI inputs are listed, never played, and no MIDI output is ever opened.
